# Cutest Picture — "Little Lantern"

Entry for make.fun `cutest-picture-01`.

A chubby baby red panda with huge starry eyes hugs a tiny glowing
mushroom in a soft pastel forest. Round shapes, big bright eyes, a head
tilt and one small glowing charm — cute at one glance, and still clear
when small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 1024 x 1024 pixels, 206,853 bytes (JPEG data),
within the 1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this picture

The judge's first test is whether the picture is cute at one glance.
One round baby red panda fills the frame: huge starry eyes, soft round
fur, a head tilt, and one small glowing mushroom held in both paws as
the charm. It is drawn cleanly, with no text and no artefacts, and the
subject stays clear even at 300 pixels across.
