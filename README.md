# Cutest Picture — "Best Friends"

Entry for make.fun `cutest-picture-01`.

A cream kitten with huge sapphire-blue eyes gently hugs a fluffy
yellow chick, wearing a tiny mint-green knitted scarf and a daisy
crown, in a soft pastel garden. Round chubby faces, one small touch of
charm in the crown and scarf — cute at one glance, and still clear when
small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 1024 x 683 pixels, 940,248 bytes, within the
1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this picture

The judge's first test is whether the picture is cute at one glance.
One cream kitten fills the frame, hugging its smallest friend: huge
blue eyes, soft round fur, a mint scarf and a daisy crown as the charm.
It is drawn cleanly, with no text and no artefacts, and the subjects
stay clear even at 300 pixels across.
