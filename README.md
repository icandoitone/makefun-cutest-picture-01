# Cutest Picture — "Otter's Little Chick"

Entry for make.fun `cutest-picture-01`.

A baby otter with big round eyes and a tiny blue bow gently hugs a
sleeping yellow chick, both wrapped together in a soft cream knitted
blanket. Two small friends cuddling, one small touch of charm in the
bow — cute at one glance, and still clear when small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 1024 x 683 pixels, 913,543 bytes, within the
1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this picture

The judge's first test is whether the picture is cute at one glance.
One baby otter fills the frame, hugging its smallest friend as they
share a blanket: big round eyes, soft fur, a blue bow as the charm.
It is drawn cleanly, with no text and no artefacts, and the subjects
stay clear even at 300 pixels across.
