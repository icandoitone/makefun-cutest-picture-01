# Cutest Picture — "Mint Scarf Friends"

Entry for make.fun `cutest-picture-01`.

A fluffy white kitten with huge sapphire-blue eyes cuddles a sleeping
yellow duckling, both wrapped together in one big mint-green knitted
scarf, a tiny daisy tucked behind the kitten's ear. Round chubby
faces, one small touch of charm in the scarf and daisy — cute at one
glance, and still clear when small.

`main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on
every run. The image is 1024 x 683 pixels, 795,781 bytes, within the
1 MiB / 1024 px limits.

License: MIT.

## Why this picture

The judge's first test is whether the picture is cute at one glance.
One white kitten fills the frame, hugging its smallest friend as they
share a scarf: huge blue eyes, soft round fur, a mint scarf and a
daisy as the charm. It is drawn cleanly, with no text and no
artefacts, and the subjects stay clear even at 300 pixels across.
